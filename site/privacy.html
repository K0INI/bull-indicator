KŌINI Bull Indicator Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026

KŌINI Bull Indicator is an informational app that displays public Bitcoin market-cycle indicators. It is designed to collect as little data as possible: none.

What we collect

Nothing. Bull Indicator has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party identifiers. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

Network requests

The app downloads market data from our public data feed (hosted on GitHub Pages) and public market APIs. These requests are ordinary HTTPS fetches; like any web server, those services may see your IP address as part of serving the request. We attach no identity to any request.

Data stored on your device

The app caches the most recent market data locally so it works offline. This cache never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is informational and appropriate for general audiences; it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
